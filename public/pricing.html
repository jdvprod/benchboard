Questions.

When does the trial end?

After your team's first finished match and its first 24 hours, whichever comes later, and never more than 14 days after you start. If you sign up on Monday for a Saturday match, you still get that match.

What happens to my stats when it ends?

Nothing is deleted. Home, Stats and your roster stay open. Tracking new matches, Film Room, scouting and AI wait until the team has a plan.

Do players and parents pay?

Not when their team has a plan: players and families join with the coach's view-only code at no charge. Anyone can follow a team on Floorcall Live for free.

Does each assistant coach need a plan?

No. One Team plan covers every coach on that team. The head coach approves each assistant who joins.

Can our school pay by invoice?

Yes. Clubs and schools can pay by card, bank transfer or purchase order. Email us and we'll send an invoice.

Why are coaches verified?

Teams include players under 18. Before a head coach can sync a team and invite players, Floorcall confirms they coach at the school or club they name.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from Settings. A yearly plan runs to the end of the year you paid for, and your stats stay yours to export.
