Questions.

When does the trial end?

After your team's first finished match and its first 24 hours, whichever comes later, and never more than 14 days after you start. If you sign up on Monday for a Saturday match, you still get that match.

What happens to my stats when it ends?

Nothing is deleted. Home, Stats and your roster stay open. Tracking new matches, Film Room, scouting and AI wait until the team has a plan.

Do players and parents pay?

Not when their coach or trainer uses FloorCall. The coach gives each family a code; entering it joins the team (or the trainer's lessons) at no charge, with Academy, Beach and the recruiting tools included. A player whose team doesn't use FloorCall can get the Player plan. Anyone can follow a team on FloorCall Live for free.

How many players does a team include?

Up to 20 on the roster, each with their family. A bigger roster, like a program that tracks JV and varsity as one team, can ask us.

Do I need an account?

Yes, for everything except FloorCall Live. One account works on every FloorCall site, and every new account starts with the free trial.

Does each assistant coach need a plan?

No. One Team plan covers every coach on that team. The head coach approves each assistant who joins.

Can our school pay by invoice?

Yes. Clubs and schools can pay by card, bank transfer or purchase order. Send us a message and we'll send an invoice.

Why are coaches verified?

Teams include players under 18. Before a head coach can sync a team and invite players, FloorCall confirms they coach at the school or club they name.

I give private lessons. Which plan is mine?

The Trainer plan: FloorCall Lessons for your sessions, open times, clinics and each athlete's progress, at $9.99 a month. Your athletes and their parents don't pay. If you also coach a team with a Team or Club plan, Lessons is included.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from Settings. A yearly plan runs to the end of the year you paid for, and your stats stay yours to export.
