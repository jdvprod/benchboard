Player statistics and sharing permission

Team:   Season:

Our coaching staff uses Benchboard to record volleyball statistics during matches and practices, plan lineups and practices, and give players feedback. The app stores your player's name, jersey number, position, graduation year and statistics. Benchboard doesn't sell this information or show ads. Its privacy policy is at the Privacy Policy link on the Benchboard website.

Please check each item you agree to:

Statistics. The coaching staff may record my player's statistics and keep them in Benchboard for coaching, practice planning and end-of-season reports.
Team pages. My player may appear on the team's live gamecast and season page, which show first name, last initial, jersey number, position and statistics to people who have the link (for example, other families).
Recruiting profile (optional). The coach may publish a recruiting profile with my player's full name, statistics and the details we provide (for example, club team, measurables, highlight video, contact email) for college coaches. It can be taken down at any time on request.

You can change your mind at any time by telling the coach. Ask the coach for a copy of your player's information or to correct or delete it.
