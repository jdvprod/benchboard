The short version
  • Benchboard is a stat-tracking tool for volleyball coaches. Coaches enter their team's roster and stats; we store it so it syncs across their devices and staff.
  • We don't sell personal information, show ads, or track you across other websites.
  • Many players are minors. Public pages show only first names, last initials and jersey numbers, unless a coach publishes a recruiting profile with the family's consent.
  • You can export or delete your data at any time from the app.

1. Who we are

Benchboard ("Benchboard", "we", "us") is operated by %LEGAL_OWNER%. This policy explains what information the Benchboard app and website collect, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. It applies to the app, the public pages it creates (live gamecasts, season pages and recruiting profiles) and this website. Questions: %LEGAL_EMAIL%.

2. Information we collect

Account information

If you create an account: your email address and password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider. We also record when you agreed to these policies and which version.

Team information you enter

Coaches and their staff enter information to run their team, which can include:

  • Team, season and schedule details, opponent names and scouting notes.
  • Roster details: player names, jersey numbers, positions, graduation year and height.
  • Match and practice statistics, lineups, rotations, film timestamps and video links, practice plans, attendance and goals.
  • Coach notes about players.
  • Recruiting profile details if a coach fills them in, which can include a player's email, phone, GPA, measurables, club team and highlight video link.

Please don't enter sensitive information such as medical or health details, government ID numbers or financial information. Benchboard isn't designed to store it.

Information stored on your device

Benchboard works offline, so it keeps your data in your browser's storage (IndexedDB and local storage) on your device. This storage is used only to run the app (your teams, settings, sign-in session and sync queue). We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't use third-party analytics.

Technical information

Like any website, the services that host Benchboard receive technical information when your device connects, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. They use it to deliver the site, keep it secure and troubleshoot.

AI features

Some features (for example "Ask your stats", report cards and timeout talking points) use an AI model. They run only when someone taps the button. When they run, a summary of the relevant team statistics (which can include player first names and jersey numbers) is sent to our AI provider to generate the answer. We count AI requests per account to enforce a daily limit.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the service: store and sync your teams, let coaches you invite work together, and create the links you choose to share.
  • To run the AI features you request.
  • To keep Benchboard secure, prevent abuse, fix problems and improve the product.
  • To contact you about your account, security or important changes.
  • To comply with law.

We don't sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or build profiles of players or students for any purpose other than running Benchboard for their team.

4. Players, minors and student data

Benchboard is meant for coaches, team staff, parents and players aged 13 and older. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly allow children under 13 to create accounts. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Coaches may enter information about players who are minors. The coach, and the school or club they coach for, decide what to enter and are responsible for having the authority and any parent or guardian consent required to do so. Our parent and guardian permission form can help.

When a school uses Benchboard, we process student information on the school's behalf and under its direction, only to provide the service. Where the U.S. Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) applies, we act as a "school official" with a legitimate educational interest. We don't sell student information, use it for targeted advertising, or use it to build profiles for non-school purposes, consistent with California's Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (SOPIPA). Parents who want to review, correct or delete their child's information should contact the coach or school; we'll help them respond.

Public pages limit what they show about players:

5. How information is shared

  • With your team: coaches and viewers you invite can see the team's information. Coaches can change it; view-only members can't.
  • Through links you create: as described above.
  • With service providers that run Benchboard for us, under contracts that limit their use of the data:
    • Supabase: database, account sign-in and storage for synced teams.
    • Cloudflare: hosting and delivery of the website and app.
    • Anthropic: AI features, only when someone uses one.
    • Google Fonts: the app loads its typefaces from Google, which receives your IP address when your browser downloads the font files.
  • For legal reasons: if required by law, or to protect the safety, rights or property of players, users, the public or Benchboard.
  • If Benchboard changes hands: information may transfer to a successor that agrees to protect it as this policy describes. We'll tell you before that happens.

6. How long we keep information

We keep account and team information while your account or team exists. When you delete players, matches, a team or your account, the information is removed from our active systems. It may remain in encrypted backups for a limited time before they are overwritten. Information stored on your device stays there until you delete it in the app or clear your browser's data.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and export: Team tab → Backup → Save all teams downloads everything as a file.
  • Correct: edit any player, match or setting in the app.
  • Delete: delete players, matches or a whole team in the app, or delete your account in Settings → Your account → Delete account. Deleting your account also deletes cloud teams you own.
  • Stop sharing: stop a recruiting profile link from the player's page, or remove a member from your team.
  • AI: AI features only run when someone taps them; you can simply not use them.

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to delete and correct it, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We don't sell or "share" personal information as California law defines those terms.

People in the EU, UK and similar places have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and to data portability, and may complain to their data protection authority. We rely on these legal bases: providing the service you asked for (contract), keeping it secure and improving it (legitimate interests), and your consent where we ask for it.

To make a request, email %LEGAL_EMAIL%. We'll verify the request comes from you (or from the coach or school responsible for a team) and respond within the time the law requires.

8. Security

Information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to team data is enforced by the database itself: only members of a team can read it, and only owners and coaches can change it. Passwords are hashed. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

9. International use

Benchboard is run from the United States and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for transfers.

10. Do Not Track

Benchboard doesn't track you across other websites, so there's nothing for browser "Do Not Track" signals to turn off.

11. Changes to this policy
