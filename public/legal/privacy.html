The short version
  • FloorCall is a set of volleyball tools: a stat and coaching app, FloorCall Live for families, Recruit, Beach, Academy and Lessons. Coaches, trainers, athletes and families put information in; we store it so it works across their devices and with the people they choose.
  • We don't sell or "share" personal information, show ads, track you across other websites, or use anyone's information to train AI models.
  • Many players are minors. Public pages show players by first name, last initial and number; full names appear only on recruiting profiles a family agreed to. Coaches can't message players privately in FloorCall.
  • You can see, export, correct and delete your information, and delete your account, at any time.

2. Information we collect

Account information (every FloorCall site)

Your email address, password (kept only in scrambled, hashed form by our sign-in provider), name, whether you joined as a coach, athlete, parent or fan, your age group (13 to 17, or 18 and over: we ask your birth month and year at sign-up but keep only the age group, never the date), whether you want news emails, and when you agreed to our terms and which version. To protect accounts, a new device must enter a code we email before it can sign in; we keep a one-way code on your device (a cookie named fc-trusted) so the same device isn't asked again. If you follow a team, we keep which teams and your notification choices.

Coach verification

Coaches who ask to be verified give their name, school or club, title, and optionally a phone number. We use it to confirm they coach there, and keep the decision and when it was made.

Team information coaches enter (coaching app)

  • Team, season and schedule details, game and practice locations, opponent names and scouting notes.
  • Roster details: player names, jersey numbers, positions, graduation year and height, and photos if a coach adds them.
  • Match and practice statistics, lineups, rotations, practice plans, attendance, goals, tryout ratings, and film: video stays on the coach's device unless the coach shares clips in a team film session, which uploads those clips.
  • Coach notes about players (private to the team's coaches), notes coaches send to the team or to players, and drills coaches send to their players.
  • Recruiting profile details if a coach fills them in with the family: for example a player's email, phone, GPA, club team, physical and strength measurements, highlight video link and a short bio.

Information players, families and staff add

  • Availability answers (yes, no, maybe) and volunteer sign-ups for team events, with the name given and which player it's for.
  • Which notes and announcements an account has opened, and which film clips a viewer has watched, so coaches can see who's caught up.
  • Which player an account belongs to (set by the team's coaches, so notes for that player reach the player and their parents).
  • Push notification subscriptions, if someone turns on match or team alerts in their browser.

FloorCall Recruit

Athletes can save their recruiting details to their account (for example name, graduation year, position, height, club and school, GPA, contact details, highlight link, and optional physical and strength numbers) so they appear on every device. Emails to college coaches are written in your browser; you copy and send them yourself, and we don't see the emails you send. Reel Studio works on your device: your video isn't uploaded. For shared recruiting profiles, we count how many times a profile was opened and when (visitors aren't identified). If college staff access is offered, staff who apply give their name, school, title and work email, which we review.

FloorCall Lessons

Trainers enter their athletes (name, graduation year, position, club, a contact detail and private notes), sessions (time, place, focus, rep counts, notes shared with the athlete, and whether a lesson was marked paid), and who booked open sessions. Athletes and parents who join with a trainer's code are linked to that athlete. If a trainer publishes a clinic sign-up page, anyone can request a spot by giving a name, a contact detail and an optional note, which only that trainer sees. FloorCall doesn't process lesson payments.

FloorCall Beach and FloorCall Academy

Beach matches are kept on the scorer's device. A shared match link carries the match inside the link; if the scorer chooses "Go live", the match is kept on our servers only while it's shared. Academy progress checklists, and which strength and training exercises you look at, are kept on your device; we don't collect health or fitness data in Academy. If your coach sends you drills, we keep which drills the team's coaches picked.

Messages to FloorCall

When you use the Contact page or a Help link, we keep your name, email, topic, message, which site you wrote from and, if you report a problem, basic device details (browser and app version) so we can help.

AI features

AI features (for example match and season reports, scouting reports, timeout talking points, report cards, film summaries and lineup or practice suggestions) run only when someone taps them. Then a summary of the relevant statistics, and notes the coaches wrote for that purpose, is sent to our AI provider to write the answer. Players appear in it by jersey number and first name, never by last name, contact details, photos or coaches' private player notes. We count AI requests per account to apply daily limits. You can hide AI features in Settings. If an account uses its own AI key, those requests go straight from the device to that provider under the account holder's agreement with it.

Information stored on your device

FloorCall works offline, so it keeps your teams, settings, sign-in session and changes waiting to sync in your browser's storage (IndexedDB and local storage) on your device. The only cookie FloorCall sets is the device check above. We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies or pixels.

Technical information

Like any website, our hosting providers receive technical information when your device connects, such as IP address, browser type and the time of the request, and use it to deliver the Service, keep it secure and troubleshoot. To stop share codes from being guessed, when a code doesn't match we keep a one-way hash of the network address for up to an hour. If the app hits an error, it can send a crash report with the error message, the screen, the app version and the browser type, and the account it happened to; never team data.

Information from other people and sources

Most information about players comes from their coaches, trainers or parents, not from the player. When a coach looks up a school's logo, the school name is searched on Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons from the coach's browser.

Please don't put sensitive information into FloorCall, such as medical, injury or health details, government ID numbers, financial account numbers or immigration status. FloorCall isn't designed to store it.

Legal bases (EU, UK and similar laws): performing our contract with you (running the Service), our legitimate interests (keeping it secure, supporting and improving it, balanced against your rights), consent (news emails, push notifications, and where we ask), and legal obligations. 4. Children, minors and student information

Accounts. FloorCall accounts are for people 13 and older. Sign-up asks for a birth month and year; if someone is under 13, they're told they can't create an account, and the browser remembers that for a day. Children under 13 may not create accounts, and we don't knowingly collect personal information directly from a child under 13. If we learn that we did, we'll delete it. If you think a child under 13 has an account, contact %LEGAL_EMAIL%. Teens 13 to 17 need a parent or guardian's permission to use FloorCall.

Players' information entered by adults. Coaches, trainers and parents may enter information about players of any age. They decide what to enter and are responsible for having the authority and any parent or guardian consent the law or their organization requires. Where a school enters information about a child under 13, it may consent on the parent's behalf for school purposes, as the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) allows. A parent or guardian can at any time ask to review their child's information, have it corrected or deleted, or stop further use of it: ask the coach, trainer or school, or contact us and we'll help.

Protections for minors built into FloorCall. Public pages show players by first name, last initial and number; a family can ask the coach to show only the number. Full names appear only on recruiting profiles the family agreed to. Coaches' announcements and notes go one way; there is no private messaging between adults and players or between players. Players can't see other players' private information. We don't show ads, sell information or allow behavioral tracking.

Schools and student data. When a school uses FloorCall, we process student information only on its behalf and under its direction. Where FERPA applies, we act as a "school official" with a legitimate educational interest. We don't sell student information, use it for targeted advertising, use it to train AI models, or build profiles for non-school purposes, consistent with California's Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (SOPIPA) and similar state laws. Our Student Data Commitments set out these promises in detail.

6. How information is shared

  • With the people you choose: your team, your trainer or athletes, and anyone you send a link to, as described above.
  • With service providers that run FloorCall for us, under contracts that limit their use of the information to providing their service:
    • Supabase: database, accounts and sign-in, file storage and server functions.
    • Cloudflare: hosting and delivery of the websites and app.
    • Resend: delivery of account, security and (if you opted in) news emails.
    • Anthropic: AI features, only when someone uses one. Anthropic doesn't train its models on this information.
    • Stripe: payments, if and when you buy a paid plan. Stripe receives your payment details directly; we don't store card numbers.
    • Browser push services (from Apple, Google or Mozilla, depending on your browser): delivering notifications you turned on.
    • Google Fonts: the websites load their typefaces from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Other services you use through FloorCall: when you play a linked YouTube or Vimeo video (YouTube is loaded in its privacy-enhanced mode), look up a logo on Wikipedia, or tap a location to open Google Maps, that service receives your request under its own privacy policy.
  • For legal and safety reasons: if required by law or valid legal process, or if we believe in good faith it's needed to protect the safety of a child or anyone else, to investigate fraud or abuse, or to protect the rights of users or FloorCall. We report apparent child sexual exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children.
  • If FloorCall changes hands: information may transfer to a successor in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets that agrees to protect it as this policy describes. We'll tell you beforehand, and student data stays subject to the Student Data Commitments.
  • With your consent, or at your direction.

We don't sell personal information, and we don't give it to data brokers or advertisers. We don't share it with third parties for their own direct marketing (California's "Shine the Light" law).

7. How long we keep information

  • Account information: while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete it along with cloud teams you own, your availability answers, sign-ups, tryouts, ratings, shared matches, notification subscriptions, note-read records, Lessons athletes and sessions you created, AI counts and messages you sent as college staff. Announcements and notes you posted to a team you don't own stay with that team without your account attached.
  • Team, Lessons and recruiting information: until the coach, trainer, family or school deletes it, or the account that owns it is deleted. Deleted information is removed from our active systems right away.
  • Crash reports and AI request counts: deleted after 90 days. Hashes from wrong share codes: deleted within an hour.
  • Messages to FloorCall: kept while we help you, then deleted within 2 years after the conversation ends, unless we need them longer for a legal reason.
  • Backups: our database provider keeps encrypted backups for a limited time (up to 30 days) so we can recover from failures. Deleted information disappears from backups as they're replaced; we don't restore backups except to recover from a failure.
  • Information on your device stays there until you delete it in the app or clear your browser's data.

9. Security

Information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is encrypted at rest by our database provider. The database itself enforces who can read or change each team's information; visitors without an account can reach only the public pages. New devices must confirm an emailed code to sign in, passwords are hashed, and the websites run only FloorCall's own code. Only a small number of FloorCall staff can reach the database, only to support users and keep the Service running. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll notify you, the affected school or organization, and regulators as the law requires, and tell you what happened and what we're doing about it.

10. Where information is processed

FloorCall is run from the United States, and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

11. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

FloorCall doesn't track you across other websites, so there's nothing for a browser's "Do Not Track" signal to turn off. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a request to opt out of sale and sharing, which we already don't do.
