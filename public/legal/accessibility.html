Accessibility

Updated %LEGAL_EFFECTIVE%

Coaches, players and parents should all be able to use Benchboard. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, the standard most schools use under Section 508 and the Americans with Disabilities Act.

What we check on every release

Automated tests run through every screen on a phone, a tablet and a computer, in light and dark mode, before each update ships. They check that:

  • every button, link and form field has a name a screen reader can announce;
  • text meets WCAG AA color contrast (4.5 to 1 for normal text, 3 to 1 for large text);
  • pages fit a phone screen without sideways scrolling, and text isn't squeezed or cut off;
  • images have text alternatives.

Ways to use Benchboard

  • Keyboard: stats can be entered entirely from a keyboard (a jersey number, then a letter for the play). Press ? on the Track screen for the list.
  • Larger text: Settings → Look → Text and button size makes everything bigger, and the app follows your device's zoom.
  • Light and dark: Benchboard follows your device's setting, or you can choose in Settings.
  • Reduced motion: animations are minimal and respect your device's reduce-motion setting.

Known limitations

  • Charts and court diagrams are visual. The numbers behind them are also shown in tables on the Stats pages.
  • The drawing boards on the Court tab can't be used with a screen reader.
  • Live tracking during a match is built for quick taps; with a screen reader, keyboard entry on a computer works best.

Tell us about a problem

If something in Benchboard is hard to use, email %LEGAL_EMAIL% with what you were trying to do and the device you were using. We'll respond and work on a fix or another way to get you the information.
