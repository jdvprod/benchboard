Answers first.

How do I get my team onto FloorCall?

Make a free account at app.getfloorcall.com, create your team and add your roster (paste a list or import a spreadsheet). FloorCall checks that you coach the team before you can invite players, usually within a day.

My coach or trainer gave me a code. Where does it go?

Make a free account, then enter the code in any FloorCall site where it asks for one, like Academy or Lessons. A team code joins you to the team; a Lessons code joins your lessons. Either way you don't pay for what your coach or trainer covers.

Does FloorCall work without internet in the gym?

Yes. Tracking works offline and syncs when you're back online. FloorCall Live needs a connection to update for families.

Can two coaches track the same match?

Yes. Each coach tracks from their own phone or laptop and the stats come together, as long as both are on the team.

Where is my game film stored?

On your own device. FloorCall doesn't upload your video; it keeps the tags, stats and clip times, and you export clips when you want to share them.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, choose "Forgot password?" and we'll email you a link to set a new one.

How do I delete my account or my child's data?

In the app, Settings → Privacy and your data → Delete your account removes it right away. For a child's data on a team, ask the coach, or send us a message about privacy and we'll take care of it.

How much does it cost?

See Pricing. Every feature is free during the beta, and teams get notice before anything is charged.
